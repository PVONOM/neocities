## Bocchi the Rock!

**"If I could be a constellation"**

Bocchi the Rock! is a 2022 Comedy Music anime about a girl named Hitori Gotoh (which is a pun cuz *'hitoribocchi'* means loner) who feels lonely and finds friends to form a band. She players the Guitar

![Season 2 Announcement](/bochi2.jpg)

### the Characters

- Gotou **(Bocchi)** Hitori
- Yamada **Ryou**
- Ijichi **Nijika**
- Ikuyo **Kita**
